# Vehicle fire or fire in a tunnel

> **In short**: stop as soon as you can do it safely, switch off the ignition, get everyone out and move at least 30 m (100 ft) away. Do not open the hood, never go back to the car, call the emergency services. In a tunnel, leave the car and walk to the nearest emergency exit, following the signs.

## When to use it

- A burning smell, smoke coming from the hood, the dashboard or the floor, a warning light with a loss of power, flames.
- A vehicle is burning ahead of you on the road.
- Smoke or fire in a tunnel.

## Steps

### Your car catches fire

1. **Stop as soon as you can do it safely**: first rest area, hard shoulder, roadside. Turn on the hazard lights.
2. **Switch off the ignition.**
3. **Get everyone out**, on the side away from traffic, children and people who move with difficulty first.
4. **Do not take your belongings.**
5. **Move at least 30 m (100 ft) away**, behind the safety barrier and clear of the traffic.
6. **Leave the hood and the trunk closed** if the fire seems to come from there: opening them feeds it.
7. **Call the emergency services.** On a motorway, use the roadside emergency phone if it is close.
8. **Never go back to the car.**

### Before you enter a tunnel

1. **Turn on your dipped headlights** and take off your sunglasses.
2. **Turn on the radio** on the frequency shown at the entrance: instructions are given over it.
3. **Keep your distance** and respect the speed limit.
4. **Check** that no warning light is on and that you have enough fuel.

### Smoke or fire in a tunnel

1. **If it is your car that is burning and the exit is close**, keep driving to get out of the tunnel.
2. **If not, pull over as far to the side as possible**, turn on the hazard lights, switch off the ignition.
3. **Put on your vest and get everyone out.** Do the same if a vehicle ahead of you is burning.
4. **Raise the alarm** from an emergency station in the tunnel.
5. **Evacuate on foot without waiting**, following the signs to the nearest way out: entrance, exit or emergency exit.
6. **If the smoke is being pushed one way**, walk to the nearest emergency exit against the airflow.
7. **Go to the assembly point** and do not go back to your car.

### Traffic jam in a tunnel

1. **Turn on the hazard lights.**
2. **Keep your distance**, even when stopped.
3. **Switch off the engine** if you remain at a standstill.
4. **Listen to the radio** and follow the signs.

## Never do this

- Go back to the car for a bag, a phone or documents.
- Open the hood to see where the smoke is coming from.
- Try to put out a burning car yourself on a motorway.
- Stay in the car in a smoke-filled tunnel.
- Make a U-turn or reverse in a tunnel.

## Depending on where you are

- **On a motorway**: wait behind the barrier, move back as far as you can, do not try to put the fire out.
- **In a French tunnel**: there is an emergency call box every 200 m (650 ft), in recesses marked in orange that also hold a fire extinguisher, and green arrows point to the nearest way out. The French Ministry of Transport says to use the extinguisher if that is possible. Do not delay the evacuation for it.
- **Carrying a can of gasoline or a gas cylinder**: tightly closed, upright, away from the passengers, with the windows open.

Sources differ on the extinguisher: the US Fire Administration and VINCI Autoroutes say not to try to put the fire out yourself, the French press release on tunnels says to use the extinguisher in the recess if that is possible. This guide keeps the most cautious line: get out and move away first. The actions for a car fire outside a tunnel come from the US Fire Administration and from VINCI Autoroutes, a French motorway operator that is not an authority. None of the sources read covers electric cars.
