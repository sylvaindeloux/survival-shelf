# Unconscious person who is breathing

> **In short**: they do not respond, but they breathe. Gently tilt their head back to open the airway, roll them onto their side (recovery position), call the emergency services, and watch their breathing without leaving them. If they stop breathing, start chest compressions.

## When to use it

- The person does not respond to your voice or touch, but their chest rises normally.
- On their back, they may choke: the tongue or fluids (saliva, blood, vomit) can block the airway.

## Steps

1. **Check that they do not respond**: shout, ask simple questions, tap or gently shake their shoulders, ask them to squeeze your hand. For a baby, tap the bottom of the foot.
2. **Shout for help** around you if you are alone.
3. **Open the airway.** They are on their back: loosen their collar, tie and belt. Put one hand on their forehead and gently tilt the head back. With the fingertips of your other hand under the bony part of the chin, lift the chin.
4. **Check for breathing**, for no more than 10 seconds: their belly and chest rise, you hear their breath, you feel it on your cheek.
   - Not breathing, or breathing that is slow, noisy or gasping: see [Cardiac arrest](cardiac-arrest.md).
   - Breathing normally: go on.
5. **Roll them onto their side**, into the recovery position (see below), except after an accident or a fall.
6. **Call the emergency services.**
7. **Watch their breathing all the time** until help arrives. Cover them: protect them from cold, heat and weather. Talk to them, they may hear you.

### The recovery position

1. Remove their glasses. Lay their legs straight, side by side.
2. Kneel beside them. Place the arm nearest to you at a right angle, elbow bent, palm up.
3. Take their other arm and hold the back of their hand against their ear, on your side. Keep your palm against theirs.
4. With your other hand, grasp the far leg behind the knee and raise it, foot on the ground.
5. Roll them toward you by pulling on that knee, until it touches the ground.
6. Gently slide your hand out from under their head, holding their elbow so that the head does not move.
7. Adjust the top leg: hip and knee at right angles.
8. Open their mouth slightly, turned toward the ground, so that fluids can drain.

## Never do this

- Leave them on their back unattended.
- Give them anything to drink or eat.
- Take your eyes off them: breathing can stop at any moment.
- Press on the soft part under the chin: lift the chin by the bone.

## Depending on where you are

- **After an accident, a fall or a blow, or when you do not know what happened**: leave them in the position you found them, call at once and follow the instructions, watching their breathing constantly. Move them only for safety, or to do chest compressions or stop heavy bleeding.
- **Baby**: turn the baby onto their side, usually in your arms. Keep the head in line with the body, without tilting it back.
- **Cold or wet ground**: slide a blanket or clothing under them if you can do it gently, and cover them.
