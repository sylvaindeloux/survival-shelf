# Cardiac arrest

> **In short**: the person does not respond and does not breathe, or breathes abnormally. Have someone call the emergency services and fetch a defibrillator. Start compressions at once: push hard and fast in the middle of the chest, 100 to 120 times a minute, without stopping. As soon as the defibrillator arrives, switch it on and follow its instructions.

## When to use it

- The person collapses and does not react to your voice or touch.
- They are not breathing, or their breathing is slow, noisy, labored or gasping.
- If in doubt, start compressions. Every minute without them lowers the chance of survival by about 10 %.

## What you need

- Your hands and a phone, nothing else.
- An automated external defibrillator (AED) if there is one nearby. Anyone can use it: it speaks and guides you.

## Steps

1. **Check**: they do not respond when you shout and tap or gently shake their shoulders. Tilt their head back and check for breathing, for no more than 10 seconds. Brief movements that look like a seizure can happen at the very start: as soon as they stop, check the breathing.
2. **Call.**
   - Someone is with you: they call the emergency number and bring back a defibrillator.
   - You are alone: call on speakerphone and start compressions while you talk.
   - No phone or no signal: go and get help, then come back and start compressions.
3. **Push.**
   - The person is on their back, on a firm, flat surface. Kneel beside them.
   - Put the heel of one hand in the middle of the bare chest, on the lower half of the breastbone. Put your other hand on top, fingers interlocked.
   - Arms straight, elbows locked, shoulders above your hands: push the chest down 5 to 6 cm (2 to 2.4 in).
   - Let the chest come back up fully between pushes, without lifting your hands.
   - Rate: 100 to 120 a minute, about 2 a second.
4. **Give breaths, if you know how.** After 30 compressions: head tilted, chin lifted, pinch the nose, seal your mouth over theirs, and blow for about 1 second, until the chest rises. Twice, in no more than 5 seconds, then 30 compressions again.
   - If you do not know how, cannot or do not feel able to: push continuously, without breaths. The operator can guide you.
5. **As soon as the defibrillator arrives**, switch it on and follow its instructions, stopping compressions as little as possible.
   - Bare and dry the chest. Stick the pads where the picture shows.
   - Nobody touches the person during the analysis or the shock.
   - Resume compressions right after the shock, or if the device says no shock is needed.
6. **Keep going** until rescuers take over, or the person breathes normally again. If there are several of you, swap about every 2 minutes.

### Child and baby

- The French reference for the public starts with 5 breaths, then alternates 15 compressions and 2 breaths. If you were trained differently, do what you were taught, and follow the operator.
- **Child**: the heel of one hand, pushing about 5 cm or 2 in (one third of the depth of the chest).
- **Baby under 1 year**: the tips of two fingers, pushing about 4 cm (1.5 in). Keep the head in line with the body, without tilting it back.
- Defibrillator: use child pads if there are some. If not, use the adult pads, one in the middle of the chest, the other in the middle of the back.
- If you cannot give breaths, push anyway.

## Never do this

- Wait for the rescuers or the defibrillator without doing compressions.
- Stop compressions to check whether the person is breathing.
- Touch the person during the analysis or the shock.
- Switch off the defibrillator or remove the pads before help arrives, even if the person seems better.

## Depending on where you are

- **Wet or metal floor**: move the person or slide a cloth under them if possible, and wipe their chest dry.
- **Medicine patch** where a pad goes: remove it. **Pacemaker**: stick the pad a hand's width away from it.
- **On a bed**: if you cannot get them onto the floor quickly, the 2025 British guidelines advise starting on the bed and pushing deeper, rather than losing time.
- **After drowning**: start with 5 breaths (see [Drowning](drowning.md)).
