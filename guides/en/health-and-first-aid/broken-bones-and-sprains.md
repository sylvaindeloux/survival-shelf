# Broken bones and sprains

> **In short**: do not move the injured part and never try to straighten it. Support it in the position that hurts least, and apply cold wrapped in a cloth for up to 20 minutes. Deformed limb, visible bone, tingling, blue or cold skin, neck or back pain: call the emergency services, and do not move the person.

## When to use it

- After a fall, a blow or a twist: sharp pain, difficulty or inability to move, swelling, deformity.
- If in doubt, act as if it were broken.

## What you need

- Something to support the limb: rolled clothing, cushions, a towel, a scarf to hold up an arm.
- Cold: an ice pack or a bag of frozen vegetables, and a cloth to wrap it in.
- A bandage for a sprain.

## Steps

1. **Firmly ask the person not to move** the injured part.
2. **Look for danger signs**: a crack was heard, the limb has changed shape or points at an odd angle, bone shows through the skin, numbness or tingling, skin that is blue, gray or cold, cannot put any weight on the foot.
3. **Call the emergency services** if you see one of these signs, or if the pain is in the neck, back, head, chest or abdomen.
4. **Support the injured part** in the position you found it, without straightening it. An arm: hold it against the chest with a towel or a scarf passed around the neck.
5. **Remove rings, watch and bracelets** before the swelling starts.
6. **If there is a wound**, cover it with a clean cloth. If it bleeds, press on it with that cloth.
7. **Apply cold** wrapped in a cloth, for up to 20 minutes, again every 2 to 3 hours. With no ice: a cloth soaked in cold water.
8. **Protect them from cold, heat and weather**, talk to them, watch them. Give nothing to eat or drink: surgery may be needed.

### Neck or back pain after an impact

- The spinal cord may be injured: ask them not to move at all, especially the head.
- Call at once.
- If you can, hold their head with both hands in the position it is in: kneel in line with the head, one hand on each side, elbows resting on the ground or on your knees.

### Blow to the head, chest or abdomen

Signs can appear later: loss of consciousness, a headache that does not go away, vomiting, agitation, drowsiness, abdominal pain. Call the emergency services.

### Sprained ankle

1. Stop the activity at once and rest the ankle.
2. Apply cold wrapped in a cloth, for about 20 minutes.
3. Put on a bandage, not too tight, until you see a doctor.
4. Raise the leg on a pillow.
5. For the first couple of days, avoid heat, alcohol and massage.

Get medical help quickly if the pain is severe, if you felt a crack or a tear, if the ankle swelled at once with bruising, or if you cannot take four steps. A simple sprain feels better in about 2 weeks; avoid strenuous exercise for up to 8 weeks.

## Never do this

- Straighten a deformed limb or push a bone back in place.
- Move a person with neck or back pain, unless there is immediate danger.
- Make them walk on a painful leg "to see if it holds".
- Put ice directly on the skin.
- Bandage too tightly: if fingers or toes turn blue, cold or numb, loosen it.

## Depending on where you are

- **Far from help**: if you have a signal, call and follow the advice. Evacuating the person yourself is rarely wise. Shelter them, insulate them from the ground and cover them. If help is more than 2 hours away, help them change position from time to time to relieve pressure points.
