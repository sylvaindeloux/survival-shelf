# Light your home without electricity

> **In short**: battery lights come before candles: flashlight, headlamp or LED lantern, with spare batteries. Keep candles as a last resort: on a stable holder, at least 30 cm (12 in) from anything that can burn, never unattended, blown out before you sleep or leave the room.

## When to use it

- Power outage, in the evening or at night.
- To get your lighting ready before the next outage.

## What you need

- An LED flashlight or headlamp per person, and a lantern for the main room.
- Spare batteries, or a hand-crank light.
- Candles, stable candle holders, a lighter or matches, as a backup.
- Everything kept in one place that you can find in the dark.

## Steps

1. **Reach for a battery light first** rather than a candle: it cannot start a fire.
2. **Find out where the outage comes from**: check your breaker panel or fuse box, and see whether the neighbors have power (see [Shut off gas, water and electricity](shut-off-gas-water-and-electricity.md)).
3. **Save light**: one lit room, where everyone gathers. Switch off as soon as you no longer need it.
4. **Keep your phone for calls**: save its battery for as long as you cannot charge it (see [Charge a phone without a power outlet](charge-a-phone-without-a-power-outlet.md)).
5. **Leave one light switched on** inside: it will tell you when the power is back.
6. **If you must light a candle**:
   1. Put it in a stable holder that cannot burn.
   2. Keep at least 30 cm (12 in) between the flame and anything that can burn: curtains, papers, bedding, decorations.
   3. Keep it out of reach of children and pets.
   4. Never leave it alone: blow it out when you leave the room.
   5. Blow out all candles before you sleep, and check that they are really out.
7. **Store matches and lighters** high up, out of reach of children.

## Never do this

- Leave a candle burning unattended, or fall asleep with a candle lit.
- Use a candle in a bedroom or anywhere someone sleeps.
- Put a candle near a curtain, papers or a decoration.
- Light a closed room with a gas or kerosene lamp or lantern made for outdoor use.
- Look for a gas leak with a flame.

## Without equipment

- No light and no candle: plan your day around daylight. Prepare the meal, the beds and whatever you will need at night before it gets dark.

## Depending on where you are

- **In an apartment**: keep a light near the front door, as the stairwell will be dark.
- **With children**: give them a battery light of their own, never a candle.
