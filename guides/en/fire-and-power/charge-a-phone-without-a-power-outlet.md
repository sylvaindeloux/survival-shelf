# Charge a phone without a power outlet

> **In short**: save the battery first: airplane or power saving mode, dim screen, texts rather than calls. To recharge: a backup battery kept full, the USB port of a laptop, or the car, always parked outside. A solar or hand-crank charger helps, slowly.

## When to use it

- A power outage that lasts.
- An evacuation, a trip or a night far from any outlet.
- To get your equipment ready before the next outage.

## What you need

- A charged backup battery (power bank), and the cable that fits your phone.
- A car charger (12 V adapter or USB port).
- If you can: a solar charger, a hand-crank radio or light with a USB output.
- Your important numbers written on paper, in case the phone dies.

## Steps

### Make the battery last

1. **Turn on power saving mode** and dim the screen.
2. **Switch off what you do not need**: Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, location services, apps open in the background.
3. **Use airplane mode** when you are not using the phone.
4. **Text rather than call**: a text often gets through when calls do not. If you must call, keep it short.
5. **If the call does not go through, wait 10 seconds** before redialing: constant redialing congests the network even more.
6. **No video, games or downloads** during the crisis.

### Recharge

1. **Backup battery**: the simplest way. Recharge it as soon as the power is back, and check it now and then: it loses its charge over the months.
2. **Laptop**: plug the phone into its USB port. It is slow and it drains the laptop, but the phone comes first.
3. **Car**: plug it into the USB port or the 12 V socket with an adapter. In some cars the engine must be running: always move the car out of the garage before you start it.
4. **Solar charger**: useful on sunny days.
5. **Hand-crank charger or radio**: it takes physical effort, but it works anywhere.

## Never do this

- Run a car engine in a garage to charge a phone, even with the door open: carbon monoxide kills.
- Drain the battery watching videos or scrolling social media.
- Rely on the phone alone: write your contacts on paper and keep a battery radio.

## Without equipment

- No way to recharge: limit phone use to what is strictly necessary.
- A corded landline phone on a traditional phone line can work without power. A cordless phone, or one plugged into an internet router, stops with the outage.

## Depending on where you are

- **At home**: keep the backup battery charged and stored with the flashlight and the radio.
- **In the car**: leave a cable and an adapter in the glove box.
- **Evacuating**: put the charger and the backup battery in the go-bag.
