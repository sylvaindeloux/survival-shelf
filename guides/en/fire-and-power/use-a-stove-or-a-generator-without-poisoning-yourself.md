# Use a stove or a generator without poisoning yourself

> **In short**: everything that burns gives off carbon monoxide, a gas you cannot see or smell and that kills. Camping stoves, barbecues and charcoal are for outdoors only, never in the home or the garage. A generator runs outside, at least 6 m (20 ft) from windows, doors and vents. Headache, nausea, dizziness: get everyone out into fresh air and call the emergency services.

## When to use it

- Power or gas outage: you want to cook, heat or make electricity another way.
- Before buying or getting out a camping stove, a space heater or a generator.

## What you need

- A battery-powered carbon monoxide detector, or one with battery backup, near every sleeping area.
- Extension cords rated for outdoor use and for the power of your appliances.
- A spot outside, away from windows, for the generator.
- The manual of each appliance.

## Steps

### Cooking

1. **Camping stove, barbecue, charcoal grill, portable burner: outdoors only.** Not in the home, nor in the garage, the basement, a shed or a crawlspace, even with windows and doors open.
2. **The gas range in your kitchen** can be used to prepare meals during a power outage. It is never used to heat the room.

### Running a generator

1. **Set it up outdoors**, outside any building: never in the home, the garage, the basement or a porch, even with the doors open.
2. **Keep it away from openings**: at least 6 m (20 ft) from any window, door or vent, with the exhaust pointing away from the home. Close the nearest windows.
3. **Plug appliances straight into the generator**, with properly rated extension cords.
4. **Never plug it into a wall outlet.** Only a qualified electrician can connect it to your panel, through an approved transfer switch.
5. **Check that your carbon monoxide detector works** before you start. Models that shut off automatically when carbon monoxide builds up are safer.

### Heating

1. **A fuel-burning space heater** (kerosene, gas) is used only if it is vented to the outside, according to the CDC. French health authorities add: never continuously, 2 hours in a row at most, in a ventilated room.
2. **Air your home for 10 minutes a day**, even in winter, and never block the air vents.
3. **Have your furnace or boiler, water heater, stove and flues checked** by a professional every year, before winter.

### Recognize poisoning and react

- **The signs**: headache, dizziness, weakness, upset stomach, vomiting, chest pain, confusion. It feels like the flu. A pet behaving strangely is also a warning. At high levels, coma comes within minutes, and people who are asleep can die before they notice anything.
- **What to do**:
  1. Get everyone out into fresh air at once.
  2. On your way out, open doors and windows and turn off the appliances if you can do it immediately.
  3. Call the emergency services from outside.
  4. Do not go back in until a professional says it is safe.
- **If the detector sounds**: leave at once for fresh air, without looking for the source, then call the emergency services.

## Never do this

- Heat your home with a gas range, an oven, a barbecue, charcoal or a camping stove.
- Burn charcoal indoors.
- Run a generator in a garage, even with the door open.
- Leave a car engine running in a garage, even with the door open.
- Sleep with a fuel-burning space heater on.
- Ignore headaches or nausea while a fuel-burning appliance is running.

## Without equipment

- No detector: apply the rules even more strictly, and at the slightest sign get out first and think afterward.
- No safe place outside for the generator or the stove: do not use them. Eat cold food and dress warmly.

## Depending on where you are

- **In an apartment**: a balcony is too close to the windows for a generator. Eat cold, or cook on your gas range if it works.
- **In a house**: choose the generator's spot in advance, far from bedroom windows and from the neighbors' windows.
- **In a tent or a shelter**: no stove or flame heater inside.
- **In a car**: to warm up or charge a phone with the engine running, the car must be outside.
