# Wash your hands without running water

> **In short**: washing your hands often is one of the key protections against disease when hygiene breaks down. Set up a washing station near the toilet and the kitchen: a water jug with a tap, soap, something to dry your hands. Scrub for 30 seconds, rinse, dry. Without water, use a hand sanitizer with at least 60 % alcohol, unless your hands are visibly dirty.

## When to use it

- **Before**: preparing or serving food, eating, feeding or breastfeeding a child, treating a wound, caring for a baby or a sick person, handling drinking water.
- **After**: using the toilet, changing a diaper, handling trash or human waste, blowing your nose, coughing or sneezing, touching an animal, contact with flood water, coming back from outside.

## What you need

- A water jug or dispenser with a tap, placed up high.
- A basin or bucket underneath, to catch the dirty water.
- Soap, liquid if possible.
- Paper towels, or one clean towel per person.
- Hand sanitizer containing at least 60 % alcohol.

## Steps

1. **Set up the washing station** next to the emergency toilet and next to where you cook: the jug up high, the basin underneath, soap and something to dry your hands beside it.
2. **Wet your hands** with a trickle of water, then close the tap.
3. **Lather and scrub for 30 seconds**: palms, backs of the hands, fingertips, nails, knuckles, wrists.
4. **Rinse** with a trickle of water.
5. **Dry** with a clean paper towel or a clean towel, or in the air.
6. **Empty the basin** of dirty water away from the water reserve and the food.

### With hand sanitizer

1. Your hands must not be visibly dirty or greasy: otherwise sanitizer works poorly, and you need soap and water.
2. Put sanitizer in your palm and rub all the surfaces of your hands for 30 seconds.
3. As soon as you have soap and water again, wash your hands: sanitizer does not remove all germs, nor chemicals.

### Which water to use

- **Tap water under a boil-water advisory**: it is usually fine for washing hands with soap, as long as you dry them well right away. Follow local advice.
- **Otherwise**: water that was boiled and cooled, or disinfected water (see [Disinfect water](../water/disinfect-water.md)).

## Never do this

- Settle for sanitizer when you have soap and water, or when your hands are visibly dirty.
- Let a child rub their eyes or put their hands in their mouth before the sanitizer is dry.
- Share one damp towel between several people.

## Without equipment

- **No jug with a tap**: one person pours a trickle of water from a bottle while the other washes.
- **No soap**: scrub with cold wood ash and water, then rinse. The WHO accepts ash as a substitute when there is no soap.
- **No water and no sanitizer**: wipes, until you find better.
