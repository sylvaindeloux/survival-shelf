# Live several days without water or electricity

> **In short**: three priorities, in this order: drinking and eating, staying warm (or cool), looking after your health. Count your water, eat what spoils first, gather in a single room, use flashlights for light and keep your hands clean. No appliance with a flame or an engine indoors. If the home becomes unlivable, leave before you are exhausted.

## When to use it

- A storm, a flood, a heat wave, extreme cold, a network failure: water and power are cut for several days.
- The emergency services give priority to the most serious cases: everyone should be able to hold out a few days on their own.

## What you need

- Water: 3 liters (about 0.8 gallons) per person per day for drinking and preparing meals, more with hygiene.
- Food that can be eaten without cooking, a manual can opener.
- Battery lamps, a battery radio, spare batteries, a power bank.
- Blankets and warm clothes.
- A bucket, strong trash bags, cat litter or sawdust, soap, hand sanitizer.
- Your medicines, a first aid kit, cash.

## Steps

### The first hour

1. **Take stock**: who is there, who needs help, which medical devices depend on power.
2. **Count your supplies**: water, food, medicines, batteries, fuel.
3. **Fill containers** while the tap still runs, and shut off the water supply if contamination is announced (see [Find water at home](../water/find-water-at-home.md)).
4. **Unplug electrical appliances** and leave one light switched on so that you see the power come back.
5. **Turn on the radio** to learn how long it is expected to last and what the instructions on water are.

### Every day

1. **Water**: drink when you are thirsty, without rationing yourself. Keep drinking water for drinking and cooking, and non-drinking water for flushing and cleaning (see [Make water safe to drink](../water/make-water-safe-to-drink.md)).
2. **Meals**: eat fresh food first, then food from the freezer, then your supplies. Eat cold if you have no safe way to cook (see [Eat without cooking](../food/eat-without-cooking.md)).
3. **Warmth**: gather everyone in one room, close the others, dress in several layers (see [Keep warm without heating](../cold-and-heat/keep-warm-without-heating.md)). In summer, close up by day and open at night (see [Cool a home without air conditioning](../cold-and-heat/cool-a-home-without-air-conditioning.md)).
4. **Light**: flashlights and lamps rather than candles. Organize the day to make the most of daylight.
5. **Toilet**: if the sewers work, flush with a bucket of non-drinking water. If not, switch to a bucket lined with bags (see [Set up an emergency toilet](../hygiene/set-up-an-emergency-toilet.md)).
6. **Hands**: wash them before eating and after using the toilet, even with little water (see [Wash your hands without running water](../hygiene/wash-your-hands-without-running-water.md)).
7. **Trash**: close the bags and take them out of the home (see [Handle household trash](../hygiene/handle-household-trash.md)).
8. **Information**: listen to the radio at set times and save your phone (see [Make your phone battery last](../communication-and-navigation/make-your-phone-battery-last.md)).
9. **Other people**: check on your elderly, sick or isolated neighbors.
10. **Morale**: keep a schedule, meals eaten together, something to keep the children busy.

### Know when to leave

1. **Leave if the home gets too cold or too hot**, if water runs short, or if a medical device needs power.
2. **Ask the local authority** about reception centers: to warm up, to charge a phone or to sleep.
3. **Before leaving in winter**, shut off the water supply and drain the pipes (see [Evacuate your home](evacuate-your-home.md)).

## Never do this

- Run a generator, a camping stove, a barbecue or a brazier in the home, the garage or the basement.
- Heat your home with the oven or the stove.
- Drink doubtful water without treating it.
- Ration drinking water.
- Leave a candle unattended.
- Wait until you are at the end of your strength to ask for help.
