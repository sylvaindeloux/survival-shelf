# Eat without cooking

> **In short**: store-bought canned food can be eaten cold, straight from the can. Add bread, crispbread or crackers, dried fruit and cereal bars, and you can last several days without lighting a flame. Eat fresh food first, try to have at least one balanced meal a day, and keep drinking.

## When to use it

- A power or gas outage, with no safe way to cook.
- Evacuation, sheltering in place, a blocked journey.
- To build a supply that depends on neither water nor fire.

## What you need

- A manual can opener.
- Cutlery, a knife, a cutting board, containers with lids.
- Soap and water for your hands, or hand sanitizer.
- A supply of ready-to-eat food, chosen from what your household already eats:
  - canned vegetables, beans, fish, meat and fruit;
  - long-life bread, crispbread, crackers, cereal, cereal bars;
  - dried fruit, nut butter, peanut butter;
  - long-life milk and juice in small cartons;
  - jars and ready-to-feed formula for a baby, food for pets;
  - a few treats: they keep spirits up.

## Steps

1. **Eat in this order**: fresh food from the fridge, then food from the freezer while it is still cold, then the supplies in the cupboard.
2. **Wash your hands** before preparing food and before eating, and use clean cutlery.
3. **Check each can** before you open it: not swollen, not rusty, not dented to the point of leaking (see [Tell whether food is still safe to eat](tell-whether-food-is-still-safe-to-eat.md)).
4. **Eat canned food as it is**: store-bought cans do not need reheating.
5. **Put together a full meal** at least once a day: a vegetable or a fruit, a starchy food, a source of protein. For example: chickpeas, tuna and crackers, then applesauce.
6. **Open only what will be eaten at the meal.** Without a fridge, leftovers do not keep more than 2 hours.
7. **Drink regularly**: eating cold does not replace water.
8. **Adapt to each person**: diet, allergies, texture for a young child or an elderly person.

## Never do this

- Eat the contents of a can that is swollen, rusty or leaking.
- Keep an open can for the next day without a fridge.
- Prepare a baby bottle with water you are not sure of.
- Let meal waste pile up in the home.

## Without equipment

- No can opener: choose pull-tab cans in advance, and keep a can opener in the go-bag.
- No water for washing dishes: eat from the can and wipe the cutlery with clean paper.

## Depending on where you are

- **If water is short**: avoid very salty, fatty or protein-rich food, which makes you thirsty. Choose salt-free crackers, whole-grain cereal and canned food with a lot of liquid.
- **With a baby**: keep ready-to-feed formula. If you have to mix powdered formula, use bottled water; boiled water is the last resort.
- **When evacuating**: bars, dried fruit and small cans weigh little and need no preparation.
