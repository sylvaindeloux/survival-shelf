# Find water at home

> **In short**: even with a dry tap, a home holds tens of liters of water: the water heater, the pipes, the ice cubes. Close your main water valve as soon as contamination is announced, to keep that water clean. The toilet tank (never the bowl) is a last resort, after treatment.

## When to use it

- The water is cut off and your reserve is running low.
- Tap water is declared unsafe and you had closed your main valve before the contamination reached you.

## What you need

- Clean containers: buckets, pots, bottles.
- A flashlight, and sometimes pliers or a screwdriver to open a drain valve.
- A way to treat the water when in doubt (see [Make water safe to drink](make-water-safe-to-drink.md)).

## Steps

1. **Close the main water valve of your home** (often near the meter) as soon as broken water or sewage lines or contamination are announced. The water already in your pipes and water heater stays clean. Find that valve today.
2. **Drink the safest water first**: bottles and stored water.
3. **Melt the ice cubes** from the freezer. Fruit juice counts as a drink too.
4. **Drain the pipes**: open the highest faucet in the home to let air in, then collect the water at the lowest faucet.
5. **Drain the water heater** (hot-water tank), which often holds 50 to 300 liters (15 to 80 gallons):
   1. Switch off its electricity at the breaker, or its gas.
   2. Close its cold-water inlet valve.
   3. Let it cool down: the water can be scalding.
   4. Open a hot-water faucet in the home to let air in.
   5. Collect the water from the drain valve at the bottom of the tank.
   6. The first liters may carry sediment: let it settle and strain it through a clean cloth.
6. **As a last resort, use the water in the toilet tank** (not the bowl), only if it holds no disinfecting or coloring block, and treat it before drinking.
7. **Keep unsafe water for everything else**: water from the bathtub, a swimming pool or a rain barrel is good for flushing and cleaning. To flush without running water, pour a bucket into the bowl in one go.

When in doubt about any of this water (cloudy, very old tank, contamination that arrived before you closed the valve), treat it before drinking.

## Never do this

- Drink water from radiators or from the boiler of a heating system: it will make you sick.
- Drink from the toilet bowl, or from a tank that holds a disinfecting block.
- Turn the electricity or the gas back on while the water heater is empty. Wait until it is full again.
- Drink water from a swimming pool or a spa: keep it for flushing and cleaning.
- Drink the water of a water bed: it contains toxic chemicals.

## Depending on where you are

- **In an apartment**: the main valve is often under the sink, in the bathroom or in a utility closet near the meter. If the building has shared hot water, you have no tank of your own: rely mostly on your reserve.
- **In a house**: think of the outdoor faucet too, which is often the lowest one, and of the well if you have one (treat that water before drinking it).
- **In the car**: never drink radiator coolant or windshield washer fluid. Both are toxic.
