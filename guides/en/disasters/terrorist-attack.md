# Terrorist attack

> **In short**: escape if you can do it without risk. Otherwise hide, lock yourself in, barricade the door, turn off the light and silence your phone, vibration included. As soon as you are safe, call the police or the emergency number. When the police arrive, come out calmly, with your hands open and visible.

## When to use it

- Gunshots, a knife attack, an explosion, a hostage-taking, a vehicle driven into a crowd, a toxic product released in a public place.
- The actions are the same whoever the attackers are and whatever their motives.

## Before

1. **Spot the exits** when you enter a public place: at least two.
2. **Report an abandoned bag or parcel at once, without touching it.**
3. **Suspicious behavior or vehicle** near a gathering place: stay discreet, remember the facts (description, license plate, direction) and call the police.
4. **Never agree** to carry a stranger's bag or parcel.
5. **Take a first aid course**: raising the alarm, chest compressions and stopping bleeding are the actions that save lives.

## During

### 1. Escape

- Only if you know where the danger is and can get away without exposing yourself.
- Leave your belongings. Take the nearest and least exposed exit.
- Help others to escape and stop people who are arriving from going in.
- Do not set off the fire alarm.

### 2. Hide, if escaping is impossible

- Lock yourself in and barricade the door with furniture.
- Turn off the light and the sound of devices. Put your phone on silent, with no vibration.
- Move away from walls, doors and windows. Lie on the floor behind solid obstacles.
- Stay silent.

### 3. Raise the alarm

- Once you are safe, call the police or the emergency number, or text if your country has a text service.
- Say where you are, what is happening, how many attackers there are and what they look like, and how many victims there are.
- Do not assume that others have already raised the alarm.

### 4. Resist, as the very last resort

- Only if you can neither escape nor hide and your life is directly threatened.
- Official instructions then say to act as a group, by surprise, and to protect yourself with whatever is at hand: a bag, a chair, a garment wrapped around your forearm.

### Depending on the form of the attack

- **Knife attack**: run. If you cannot, put an object between the attacker and you: a bag, a chair.
- **Explosion**: move away, touch no object or debris, shelter behind a solid obstacle. A second explosion is possible.
- **Hostage-taking**: do not provoke the attackers, obey their instructions.
- **Train or subway**: hide under the seats. Get out only if you do not have to cross the tracks.
- **Toxic product** (an abnormal smell, several people crying or feeling faint in the same place): cover your nose and mouth with a cloth, leave the place quickly while helping the most vulnerable, do not sit or lie down. Once you are away, take off your outer layer of clothing without touching the outside and wash your hands. Wait for the emergency services on the spot: do not go home and do not go to the hospital on your own.
- **Vehicle driven into a crowd**: the official documents read for this guide give no instruction specific to this case. The same principles apply: move away, shelter behind a solid obstacle, raise the alarm.

### When the police arrive

1. Stay locked in until they come to evacuate you.
2. Come out calmly, with your hands empty, open and clearly visible, so that you are not taken for a suspect. Do not run toward them.
3. Follow their orders. Their first task is to stop the threat: they may walk past injured people without stopping.
4. Tell them about the injured and where they are.

## After

1. **Help the injured** if you are safe: press hard on a bleeding wound (see [Stop severe bleeding](../health-and-first-aid/stop-severe-bleeding.md)).
2. **Stay available to the investigators**: your testimony matters.
3. **Follow official accounts** and do not pass on rumors. If you took photos or videos of the attack, keep them for the authorities.
4. **After a toxic product**, serious symptoms can appear several hours later: call the emergency services and say that you were in the area.
5. **Take care of yourself and of the children**: avoid watching the images over and over, and ask for help if the shock does not pass (see [Shock, stress and supporting others](../health-and-first-aid/shock-stress-and-supporting-others.md)).

## Never do this

- Stay to watch or film when you can escape.
- Set off the fire alarm: the reaction expected is not the same as for a fire.
- Run toward the police, or come out with an object in your hand.
- Touch an abandoned bag.
- Pass on rumors, or post your photos and videos of the attack instead of keeping them for the authorities.

## Depending on where you are

- **In France**: call 17 or 112, or text 114. On a train or in the subway, call 31 17 or text 31 177.
