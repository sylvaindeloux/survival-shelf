# Industrial accident and toxic cloud

> **In short**: at the siren or the alert on your phone, go into the nearest building at once, close doors, windows and vents, turn off the ventilation and listen to the radio. No flame, no spark. Do not fetch your children from school, and go out only when the alert is over.

## When to use it

- An accident in a factory, a warehouse or a depot of dangerous products.
- An accident involving a truck, a train or a ship carrying hazardous materials.
- Smoke, a cloud or an abnormal smell, a warning siren, an alert on your phone.
- You can be exposed without seeing or smelling anything: trust the alert.

## What you need

- A battery radio.
- Wide tape and cloths to block openings.
- Water and your emergency kit.

## Before

1. **Find out** from your local authority whether there is a hazardous site near your home, your workplace or the school.
2. **Learn the warning signal** used where you live, and the all-clear.
3. **Choose the room where you will shelter**: the one with the fewest openings, upstairs if possible, because some gases are heavier than air.
4. **Write down the frequency** of your local public radio station.

## During

1. **Go into the nearest building.** In a car, park and get to a building: a vehicle does not protect you.
2. **Close doors and windows.** Block the vents and the bottom of the doors, and turn off the ventilation, the heating and the air conditioning.
3. **Stay away from the windows.**
4. **No flame, no spark**: do not smoke, do not light anything.
5. **Trouble breathing**: breathe through a damp cloth.
6. **Listen to the radio** and follow the instructions of the authorities.
7. **Leave your children at school**: the teachers shelter them.
8. **Phone only for a life-threatening emergency**, to leave the networks to the emergency services.
9. **Evacuate only when ordered**: take the essentials and your medicines, and follow the route you are given, with no shortcut.

### You are outside, near the accident

- Move away from the area. If you cannot, go into a closed building close by.
- Protect your nose and mouth with a cloth, damp if possible.

### The product has touched you

1. Once you are away from it, take off your outer layer of clothing without touching the outside, and put it in a plastic bag.
2. Wash your hands with soap and water.
3. Do not drink, eat or smoke, and do not touch your face.
4. Wait for the emergency services on the spot rather than going to the hospital on your own: you could contaminate other people.
5. See [Chemical poisoning](../health-and-first-aid/chemical-poisoning.md).

## After

1. **Wait for the all-clear**: the end-of-alert signal or an announcement by the authorities.
2. **Then air** the whole home.
3. **Symptoms can appear several hours later**: call the emergency services and say that you were in the area.
4. **Follow the instructions** given afterwards by the authorities.

## Never do this

- Stay outside or at the window to watch or film.
- Stay in your car.
- Fetch your children from school.
- Light a flame or smoke.
- Go out before the all-clear.

## Depending on where you are

- **Near a hazardous site**: ask your local authority for the instructions specific to the site and keep them at hand.
- **On the road, behind a crashed truck**: do not go near it, move away and call the emergency services.
- **In France**: the national warning signal is three sequences of 1 minute 41 seconds, 5 seconds apart. The all-clear is a continuous 30-second sound. The town hall and the Géorisques website list the hazardous sites.
