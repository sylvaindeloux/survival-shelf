# Tornado

> **In short**: take shelter at once on the lowest level of a sturdy building, in a small interior room with no windows, and protect your head and neck. A car, a trailer or a mobile home does not protect you. Never shelter under a bridge.

## When to use it

- A violent thunderstorm, especially in the late afternoon or early evening.
- You see or hear one of these signs:
  - a rotating funnel-shaped cloud, or an approaching cloud of debris;
  - a continuous roar, like a freight train;
  - large hailstones: they often come with tornadoes.
- Some tornadoes have no visible funnel: if you feel in danger, take shelter without waiting.

## Before

1. **Pick your shelter**: a basement or a storm cellar. Otherwise a small interior room on the ground floor: bathroom, closet, hallway.
2. **Pick a sturdy building nearby** if you live in a trailer or a mobile home.
3. **Follow the thunderstorm and tornado warnings** of your weather service.

## During

1. **Go down to the lowest level**, into an interior room. Close doors and windows and stay away from glass and outside walls.
2. **Protect your head and neck** with your arms. Cover yourself with a blanket or a coat.
3. **In an apartment building or an office**: an interior hallway or room, as low as possible. Do not take the elevator.
4. **In a large building** (gym, shopping mall): avoid the large halls. Get under something solid, a table or a desk.
5. **In a trailer, a mobile home or a tent**: get out and go to a sturdy building at once.

### In a car or outside

- Do not try to outrun a tornado in a car.
- If it is still far away, in open country, drive at right angles to its path, toward a sturdy building.
- If it is upon you and there is no shelter: either stay in the car, down low, with your head covered; or get out and lie in a ditch or a hollow, protecting your head. Watch for water that may rise there.

## After

1. **Stay sheltered** until the warning ends.
2. **Do not go into damaged buildings.**
3. **Smell of gas or a spilled product**: get out at once, without lighting a flame or touching a switch.
4. **If you are trapped**: cover your mouth with a cloth, send a text message, bang on a pipe or a wall, or whistle rather than shout.
5. **Stay away from fallen wires.** Wear thick-soled shoes, long pants and gloves: nails and glass are everywhere.
6. **Do not move an injured person** who has back or neck pain, unless there is an immediate danger.

## Never do this

- Stay near a window to watch or film.
- Shelter under a bridge or an overpass: a low, flat spot is safer.
- Stay in a car or a mobile home when there is a sturdy building nearby.
- Use a lighter or matches in a damaged building.
