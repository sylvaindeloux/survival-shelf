# Network and payment outage

> **In short**: when the phone, the internet or bank cards stop working, get your news from a battery radio, pay in cash, send short text messages rather than calling, and meet your relatives at the place you agreed on beforehand. Prepare all this before: afterwards it is too late.

## When to use it

- The mobile network, the internet or the landline is down.
- Cash machines are empty or switched off, card terminals are out of service.
- Often after a power outage, a storm or a cyberattack.

## What you need

- A battery or hand-crank radio, with the frequencies of the public radio written on it.
- Cash, in small bills.
- A list of contacts on paper.
- A charged power bank.
- A paper road map of your area.

## Before

1. **Keep a little cash at home**, enough to pay for essentials for a few days. Keep it in a safe place, away from the entrances, and do not talk about it.
2. **Write your important contacts on paper**: relatives, doctor, local authority, insurer.
3. **Agree with your relatives** on a meeting place and on a distant contact who will act as a relay (see [Make a household plan](../basics/make-a-household-plan.md)).
4. **Keep paper copies** of your important documents (see [Keep your documents and contacts safe](../basics/keep-your-documents-and-contacts-safe.md)).
5. **Keep your car's tank** at least half full: without power or payment, the pumps stop.

## During

### Keeping informed

1. **Listen to the public radio** on a battery set or in the car.
2. **Rely on official channels**: local authorities, emergency services. Without the internet, go to the town hall.
3. **Be wary of rumors.** Before you believe or repeat a piece of news, ask yourself who issued it, whether the source is official, whether a major news outlet has carried it, and who benefits from it.

### Communicating

1. **Send short text messages** rather than calling: they get through better on a congested network.
2. **If you must call, keep it brief.** If the call fails, wait about ten seconds before redialing.
3. **Save your battery**: lower the brightness, close the apps, switch to airplane mode between attempts.
4. **Emergency with no network**: see [Call the emergency services](../basics/call-the-emergency-services.md). You can also go straight to the police station or the town hall.
5. **Meet your relatives** at the agreed place. Call on your neighbors, especially people who live alone.

### Paying

1. **Pay in cash** and keep your reserve for essentials: water, food, medicines, fuel.
2. **Do not count on cash machines**: they depend on power and on the network, and they are mobbed as soon as they work again.
3. **Buy only what you need**, so that everyone can get supplies.

## After

1. **Check your bank statements** and report any transaction you do not recognize.
2. **Be wary of messages** asking for your codes "following the outage": your bank never asks for them (see [Cyberattack, scams and disinformation](cyberattack-scams-and-disinformation.md)).
3. **Rebuild your cash reserve** and update your contact list.

## Never do this

- Call the same number again and again: you congest the network.
- Pass on news you have not checked.
- Give a bank code or a password to someone who calls you.
- Tell people where you keep your money.

## Depending on where you are

- **Away from home**: carry a little cash, a paper map and a relative's number written down somewhere.
- **With children**: teach them their full name, your name and your number, and slip these on paper into their bag.
