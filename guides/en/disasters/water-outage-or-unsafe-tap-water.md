# Water outage or unsafe tap water

> **In short**: keep a reserve of bottled water. Read the advisory carefully: "boil water" means 1 minute at a rolling boil before drinking, cooking or brushing your teeth; "do not drink" means boiling is useless and you need bottled water; "do not use" means bottled water for everything. When the water comes back, let it run until it is clear.

## When to use it

- The tap is dry, whatever the cause.
- The authorities or your water supplier announce that the water is no longer safe to drink.
- Tap water is cloudy, colored or smells bad after a flood, a cyclone or an outage.

## What you need

- Bottled water: the French Government advises 6 liters (about 1.5 gallons) per person for 3 days.
- A way to boil water without electricity: a camping stove and fuel.
- Clean containers or buckets with lids.
- Soap and hand sanitizer.

## Before

1. **Build your water reserve** (see [Store water](../water/store-water.md)).
2. **Know where the information will come from**: local authority, local radio, water supplier.
3. **When an outage is announced**, fill clean containers for drinking, and others, kept for that use only, for washing and for the toilet. Cover them: standing water attracts mosquitoes.

## During

### The tap is dry

1. **Keep bottled water for drinking** and cooking.
2. **Look for the water left in your home** (see [Find water at home](../water/find-water-at-home.md)).
3. **Save the water used for washing and for the toilet** (see [Wash yourself with little water](../hygiene/wash-yourself-with-little-water.md) and [Set up an emergency toilet](../hygiene/set-up-an-emergency-toilet.md)). Water that is not safe to drink is good enough to flush.
4. **Ask about the water distributions** organized by the local authority.
5. **Do not keep water drawn from the tap during the crisis for too long**: the health agency of Mayotte (France) says not to drink it after 48 hours.

### "Boil water" advisory

1. **For drinking, cooking and brushing your teeth**: bottled water, or water kept at a rolling boil for 1 minute and then cooled (see [Make water safe to drink](../water/make-water-safe-to-drink.md)). If the local advisory gives another time, follow it: in Mayotte it is 5 minutes.
2. **Boil even filtered water**: filter jugs and under-sink filters do not make water safe to drink.
3. **Baby bottles**: bottled water, or ready-to-use formula if possible.
4. **Hands**: tap water and soap are usually fine.
5. **Shower**: possible, without swallowing water. For babies and young children, prefer a sponge bath.
6. **Dishes**: see [Wash clothes and dishes with little water](../hygiene/wash-clothes-and-dishes-with-little-water.md). **Laundry**: as usual.
7. **Pets**: bottled water, or boiled and cooled water.
8. **Do not use** the water or the ice from the refrigerator dispenser.

### "Do not drink" advisory

- **Boiling does not make this water safe**: the pollution is chemical.
- Bottled water for drinking, cooking, washing fruit and vegetables, preparing baby bottles, making ice, brushing your teeth and watering pets.
- For other uses, follow the local advisory.

### "Do not use" advisory

- Bottled water for everything. Mere contact of this water with the skin, the eyes or the lungs can be dangerous.

## After

1. **Wait until the advisory is officially lifted.**
2. **Let the water run** until it is clear: after an outage it often comes out cloudy or colored.
3. **Follow the local instructions for the return of the water.** In Mayotte, for example, the health agency asks people to boil the water for 6 hours after an overnight outage and for 12 hours after a 24-hour outage.
4. **Throw away ice** made during the period and empty any doubtful water reserves.
5. **Diarrhea, vomiting, fever or stomach pain** after drinking the water: see a doctor or call the emergency services (see [Dehydration and diarrhea](../health-and-first-aid/dehydration-and-diarrhea.md)).

## Never do this

- Drink "do not drink" water after boiling it.
- Brush your teeth or prepare a baby bottle with doubtful water.
- Rely on a filter jug to make water safe to drink.
- Drink rainwater without treating it.
- Store drinking water in a container that has held something else.

## Depending on where you are

- **In the tropics after a cyclone or heavy rain**: prefer bottled water until the authorities have confirmed that the water is safe (see [Cyclone and hurricane](cyclone-and-hurricane.md)).
- **With a private well** after a flood: have the water tested before you drink it.

The Mayotte agency also advises against adding bleach to drinking water, while other sources accept it when boiling is not possible: see [Disinfect water](../water/disinfect-water.md). The delays given for Mayotte are specific to its network and are quoted as an example.
