# Volcanic eruption

> **In short**: follow the instructions of the authorities and evacuate as soon as they ask: it is the only protection against lava flows, flying debris and burning gases. Under ash or gas, stay indoors with doors and windows closed, and breathe through a mask or a damp cloth.

## When to use it

- You live or travel near an active volcano.
- The authorities announce an eruption, or a cloud of ash or gas.

## What you need

- Filtering masks (N95 or FFP2 type): they help against ash, not against gases.
- Goggles, long sleeves and long pants.
- Bottled water, a battery radio.
- Tape and cloths to seal openings.

## Before

1. **Find out**: are you in a risk zone? What are the evacuation routes and the reception centers?
2. **Pack your go-bag** and plan what you will do with your animals.
3. **Follow the bulletins** of the authorities as soon as the volcano becomes restless.

## During

1. **Evacuate as soon as the authorities ask**, by the routes they give.
2. **Avoid the valleys and rivers** below the volcano: mudflows follow them. Avoid the areas downwind too.
3. **Cloud of ash or gas: stay indoors.** Close and strengthen doors, windows and shutters. Seal the openings and stop the ventilation.
4. **Protect your breathing** with a mask or a damp cloth over your nose and mouth.
5. **Protect your skin and eyes**: long sleeves, long pants, goggles. Ash irritates the skin, the eyes and the airways.
6. **Do not stay in low-lying spots**: gases collect there.
7. **Avoid driving in ash**: visibility drops, the road is slippery and the engine clogs.
8. **Listen to the radio** and be ready to evacuate.

## After

1. **Wait for the authorities' agreement** before going out or going home.
2. **Trouble breathing**: stay indoors.
3. **Be wary of water**: ash can make water unfit to drink. Drink bottled water until the tap water is confirmed safe.
4. **Throw away food** exposed to ash, heat or fumes.
5. **Protect yourself to clean up**: mask, gloves, long sleeves, goggles. Wet ash is slippery.
6. **Do not go up on the roof** to remove ash without guidance or equipment: it is slippery and can give way under the weight.

## Never do this

- Go and watch the eruption or the flows.
- Stay in an evacuated zone "to look after the house".
- Cross a valley or a river below the volcano.
- Rely on a mask to protect you from gases.
