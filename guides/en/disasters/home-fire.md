# Home fire

> **In short**: smoke kills more than flames. The fire is in your home and you cannot put it out at once: get everyone out, close the door of the burning room and then the front door, go down by the stairs and call the emergency services once you are outside. The fire is elsewhere and there is smoke in the stairwell: stay in your home, with the door closed and sealed, and show yourself at the window.

## When to use it

- Flames, smoke or a smoke alarm sounding in your home or in the building.
- To prepare your household: you may have as little as two minutes to get out.

## What you need

- A smoke alarm on every level, near the bedrooms.
- A fire extinguisher within reach, if you know how to use it.
- Cloths that you can wet.

## Before

1. **Install smoke alarms** and test them every month.
2. **Make an escape plan** with the whole household and practice it twice a year.
3. **Keep anything that burns away** from heat sources: paper, cardboard, fabrics, alcohol, gasoline.
4. **Never leave a candle unattended.** Keep matches and lighters out of children's reach.
5. **Never use alcohol or gasoline** to light a fire or a barbecue.

## During

### The fire is in your home

1. **Try to put out only a very small fire**, without taking any risk (see [Put out a small fire](../fire-and-power/put-out-a-small-fire.md)).
2. **Otherwise, get everyone out**, taking nothing with you.
3. **Close the door of the burning room, then the front door**, without locking it: a closed door holds back fire and smoke.
4. **Leave by the stairs**, never by the elevator.
5. **Call the emergency services** as soon as you are safe.
6. **Never go back inside.**

### The fire is elsewhere in the building

- **Smoke in the hallway or the stairwell, fire below you or on your floor: stay in your home.** Close the door, wet it and seal it with damp cloths, call the emergency services and show yourself at the window.
- **Fire above your home**: leave by the nearest exit.

### There is smoke

- Do not go through smoke: where there is smoke, do not go.
- If you are caught in it, get down close to the floor, with a damp cloth over your nose and mouth.

### Your clothes catch fire

- Stop, drop to the ground and roll over and over to smother the flames.

## After

1. **Let the firefighters in** and tell them if someone is missing.
2. **Go back in only when they allow it.**
3. **Call them even if the fire seems to be out**: it can smolder, for example in an extractor hood duct.
4. **A burn or trouble breathing**: call the emergency services.

## Never do this

- Fight the fire alone without having the emergency services called.
- Take the elevator.
- Walk into smoke.
- Jump out of the window.
- Go back for an object or an animal.
- Throw water on burning oil.

## Depending on where you are

- **In the kitchen**: a pan or a fryer on fire, turn off the gas or the hotplate and put a lid on. Do not move the pan.
- **An electrical appliance on fire**: cut the power at the breaker and call the fire service.
- **In France**: smoke alarms must meet the NF EN 14604 standard. Call 18 or 112.
