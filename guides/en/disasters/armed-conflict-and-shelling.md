# Armed conflict and shelling

> **In short**: at the siren or the first explosions, take shelter at once: in a basement if possible, otherwise in a room separated from the outside by at least two walls, away from windows. Caught outside, lie down and protect your head. Never touch an unknown object: it may explode. Evacuate only when told to, by the routes you are given.

## When to use it

- Air strikes, artillery fire or fighting in your region or where you are staying.
- A threat of war announced by the authorities.
- For violence between armed groups inside the country, see also [Civil war](civil-war.md).

## What you need

- Supplies for 3 to 7 days: water (about 3 liters, or 3 quarts, per adult per day), food that can be eaten without cooking, medicines.
- A battery radio, flashlights, a power bank.
- Your documents in a waterproof pouch, and digital copies of them.
- Cash in small bills, a paper road map.
- A first aid kit.

## Before

1. **Choose your shelter**: a cellar or a basement. Failing that, a room separated from the outside by at least two walls, away from windows.
2. **Find the nearest shelters** to the places where you spend time: basements, brick or concrete buildings.
3. **Prepare your documents**: identity papers, family record, birth certificates, insurance, property deeds.
4. **Prepare the children**: have them learn their full name, their parents' names and a phone number, and give them this information on a piece of paper to keep on them.
5. **Agree with your relatives** on how you will find each other: the phone may stop working (see [Make a household plan](../basics/make-a-household-plan.md)).
6. **Learn how to stop bleeding** (see [Stop severe bleeding](../health-and-first-aid/stop-severe-bleeding.md)).

## During

### Siren, strikes or gunfire

1. **Go to the nearest shelter at once.**
2. **Indoors**: go down to the basement. Otherwise put at least two walls between you and the outside and stay away from windows.
3. **Lie down**, wrap your arms around your head, cover your ears and keep your mouth open: this reduces the effect of the blast on the body.
4. **Wait until the firing stops** before moving to a better shelter.

### You are outside

1. **Lie down and protect your head.**
2. **Reach a shelter by crawling or in short dashes**, using buildings and solid obstacles for cover.
3. **Aim for a basement or a brick or concrete building.**

### Keeping informed

1. **Listen to the radio and official channels.** At the siren, turn on the radio and wait for instructions.
2. **Check before you share**: who is the author, when was it published, where does it come from, what is its purpose? Manipulating information is part of conflicts.
3. **Do not phone unless you have to**: leave the lines to the emergency services.

### Evacuating

1. **Leave your home only when the authorities say so**, unless the home itself becomes dangerous.
2. **Listen for the route, the destination and the assembly point.** Take the recommended roads, not ones you do not know.
3. **Take** your bag, your documents, your medicines. Tell your relatives.
4. **Before leaving**: turn off the electrical appliances you do not need, close the windows, lock the doors.

## After

1. **Touch no unknown object**, even one that looks harmless: unexploded shells, grenades and ammunition stay dangerous long after the fighting and can explode without warning. Move away, keep children at a distance and report the object to the authorities.
2. **Do not go into a damaged building.**
3. **Care for the injured**: press directly on a bleeding wound with a clean cloth.
4. **You have lost contact with a relative**: the Red Cross and Red Crescent can start a search, free of charge, through their Restoring Family Links service.
5. **Chemical or nuclear attack**: see [Industrial accident and toxic cloud](industrial-accident-and-toxic-cloud.md) and [Nuclear explosion](nuclear-explosion.md).

## Never do this

- Stand at the window to watch or film.
- Pick up or move an unknown object, ammunition or debris from a weapon.
- Go out during the firing.
- Post the position of soldiers, rescuers or strikes.
- Pass on unverified information.
