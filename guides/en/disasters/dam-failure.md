# Dam failure

> **In short**: at the dam warning signal, go to the nearest high ground immediately. If you cannot, go up to the upper floors of a tall, solid building. Do not turn back and do not wait to see the water.

## When to use it

- You live, work or camp in a valley downstream of a large dam.
- The warning signal specific to dams sounds, or the authorities announce a danger at the dam.

## Before

1. **Ask your local authority** whether you are in the area the water would reach.
2. **Learn the signal** used for the dam, and the all-clear.
3. **Find the high points** nearest to your home, your workplace and the school: hills, upper floors of solid buildings. Find the paths to walk there too.
4. **Prepare your emergency kit** and keep it near the exit.
5. **Sign up for the alerts** of your local authority and follow the flood warnings.

## During

1. **Leave at once for the nearest high point.** Every minute counts.
2. **If you cannot reach it**, go up to the upper floors of a tall, solid building.
3. **Take your kit and your medicines** if they are within reach. Do not look for anything else.
4. **Do not turn back.**
5. **Move away from the valley bottom** and from the river.
6. **Leave your children at school**: the school applies its own sheltering plan.
7. **Stay on high ground** until the all-clear signal or until the authorities say so.

## After

1. **Follow the instructions** of the authorities before coming back down.
2. **Avoid walking in flooded areas.**
3. **Back home**: air the home, disinfect with bleach and heat it as soon as possible.
4. **Turn the power back on** only when the wiring is dry. If in doubt, have the installations checked.
5. **Check that the tap water is safe** before you drink it.

## Never do this

- Wait to see the water coming.
- Go down to the river to look.
- Go back for belongings, a vehicle or an animal.
- Take refuge in a basement or an underground garage.

## Depending on where you are

- **On vacation or camping near a river**: ask on arrival whether there is a dam upstream and where the high point is.
- **By the water downstream of a dam**: obey the no-entry signs and the flood warnings.
- **In France**: the dam signal is a foghorn, 2 seconds of sound then 3 seconds of silence, for at least 2 minutes. The all-clear is a continuous 30-second sound. These sirens are tested every quarter: in the Nièvre department, for example, on the first Wednesday of March, June, September and December at 12:15 p.m.
