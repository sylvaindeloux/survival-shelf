# Thunderstorm, lightning and hail

> **In short**: as soon as you hear thunder, lightning is close enough to strike you. Go into a solid building or a closed car, and wait 30 minutes after the last thunderclap. Outside, no place is safe: never under a lone tree, on high ground or in the water.

## When to use it

- Orange or red weather warning for thunderstorms, a darkening sky, thunder.
- Any outdoor activity: hiking, swimming, sports, work in the fields.

## Before

1. **Check the forecast** and put off outdoor activities if thunderstorms are expected.
2. **Pick a shelter**: a solid building, or failing that a car with a metal roof.
3. **Bring in or tie down** what the wind can carry away. Put vehicles under cover from hail if you can.
4. **Move away in advance** from rivers and low points: a thunderstorm can flood basements and streets within tens of minutes.

## During

### Indoors

1. Stay away from windows and doors.
2. **Avoid appliances plugged into the mains** and corded phones.
3. **Avoid tap water**, showers and baths: lightning can travel through the plumbing.
4. Do not lie on a concrete floor or lean against a concrete wall.

### Outdoors

1. **Walk to a shelter without waiting.** A porch, an open hut or a grandstand does not protect you.
2. **Get off high ground**: hills, ridges, summits. Avoid open spaces: stadiums, fields, beaches.
3. **Never shelter under a lone tree**, or under a rock overhang. Lightning can also spread through the ground around a tree.
4. **Get out of the water** and move away from ponds, lakes and pools.
5. **Keep away from anything that conducts electricity**: fences, pylons, railings, an umbrella.
6. Never lie flat on the ground.

### In a car

- A car with a metal roof and closed windows protects you from lightning. Do not touch the metal parts.
- Never drive onto a flooded road or bridge.
- Under a red warning: drive at walking pace, park safely away from trees and stay in the vehicle.

### Hail

- Take shelter: large hailstones can injure you or knock you out.

## After

1. **Wait 30 minutes** after the last thunderclap before going back out.
2. **Stay away from fallen wires** and report them.
3. **Beware** of weakened roofs, chimneys and trees.
4. **Someone is hurt**: call the emergency services.

## Never do this

- Shelter under a tree: it is the leading cause of death by lightning.
- Stay in the water or on a boat.
- Stay in a basement or an underground garage that is flooding.
- Go out between two thunderclaps because the rain has stopped.

## Depending on where you are

- **In the mountains**: come down from ridges and summits at the first signs. Do not shelter under a rock face.
- **On a boat or swimming**: get to land and to a shelter at once.
- **In a forest in dry weather**: lightning can start a fire. See [Wildfire](wildfire.md).
