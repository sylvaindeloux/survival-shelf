# Storm and high winds

> **In short**: get inside a solid building before the gusts arrive, close doors, windows and shutters, and stay away from the glass. Outside, the danger comes from trees and objects that fall or fly. Do not go up on the roof and never go near a fallen power line.

## When to use it

- Orange or red weather warning for high winds.
- A storm is forecast, or the gusts are picking up.

## What you need

- A flashlight and a battery radio, with spare batteries: power and phone outages can last several days.
- Your emergency kit, water and food you can eat without cooking.
- A charged phone and power bank.

## Before

1. **Follow the warnings** of your weather service and the instructions of the local authorities.
2. **Bring in or tie down anything that can fly away**: garden furniture, trash cans, pots, tools. Carried by the wind, they become projectiles.
3. **Prune the trees** close to the house and clean the gutters, well before the storm season.
4. **Park your car** away from trees.
5. **Get ready for a power outage**: flashlights, radio, charged phone (see [Long power outage](long-power-outage.md)).

## During

1. **Stay inside a solid building.** Under a red warning, stay home and do not use your car.
2. **Close doors, windows and shutters**, and stay away from the glass.
3. **Move to the lowest level**, into a small interior room with no windows, if the wind becomes very violent.
4. **Limit your trips.** If you must go out, watch for falling trees, roof tiles and objects, and avoid roads through forests.
5. **Do not go up on the roof** and do not touch wires lying on the ground.
6. **Run a generator outdoors**, never in the home, the garage or the basement.

### If you are caught in a car or outside

- Get to a sturdy building and go inside.
- Do not shelter under a tree or under a bridge.
- In a car, gusts push the vehicle sideways: slow down and stop away from trees and power lines.

## After

1. **Stay at least 10 m (35 ft) away from fallen power lines** and from anything they touch. Report them.
2. **Beware of what is still standing**: weakened roofs, chimneys, tiles and trees can fall after the storm.
3. **Leave repairs at height** to professionals.
4. **Use a flashlight**, not candles.
5. **Throw away food** that stayed too long without refrigeration (see [Long power outage](long-power-outage.md)).
6. **Check on** your neighbors and on isolated people.

## Never do this

- Go out to repair a roof or an antenna during the storm.
- Walk in a forest or along the coast.
- Touch a fallen wire, or a branch that touches it.
- Run a generator or a camping stove indoors.

## Depending on where you are

- **Trailer, mobile home, tent**: they do not protect you from high winds. Get to a solid building before the storm.
- **On the coast**: when a warning for waves and coastal flooding is issued, do not go out to sea and keep away from the shore and estuaries. The sea can flood the shore at high tide.
- **In the tropics**: see [Cyclone and hurricane](cyclone-and-hurricane.md).
