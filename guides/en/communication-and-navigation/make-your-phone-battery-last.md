# Make your phone battery last

> **In short**: as soon as the outage starts, switch to power-saving mode, lower the brightness and turn off Wi-Fi, Bluetooth and location. Between two uses, switch to airplane mode. Write short texts instead of calling, forget videos, and get your news from the radio instead of the phone. Keep the battery for emergencies.

## When to use it

- A power outage that lasts, an evacuation, a blocked journey, a trip far from any outlet.
- You have only a power bank, or nothing, to recharge.
- Before a forecast storm, to start with full batteries.

## What you need

- A fully charged phone and its cable.
- A charged power bank, a car charger.
- A battery radio, so that you do not use the phone for news.
- Your important numbers on paper, in case the phone dies.

## Steps

### Before the outage

1. **Charge everything** as soon as a storm or an outage is announced: phones, power banks, rechargeable lamps.
2. **Keep a cable and a charger in the car.**
3. **Write down on paper** the numbers you would need if the phone died.

### As soon as the outage starts

1. **Turn on power-saving mode.**
2. **Lower the screen brightness.**
3. **Turn off Wi-Fi, Bluetooth and location**, and close the apps running in the background.
4. **Switch to airplane mode** between two uses. Turn the network back on at times agreed with your family to pick up messages.
5. **If there are several of you**, use one phone at a time and keep the others switched off.

### While it lasts

1. **Write instead of calling**: a short text gets through better than a call on an overloaded network. If you have to call, keep it brief.
2. **The call does not go through**: wait 10 seconds before redialing, instead of calling again and again.
3. **No videos, games or downloads.**
4. **Get your news from a battery radio** or the car radio, not from the phone.
5. **Use a flashlight**, not the phone, for light.
6. **Recharge whenever you can**: power bank, USB port of a laptop, car parked outdoors (see [Charge a phone without a power outlet](../fire-and-power/charge-a-phone-without-a-power-outlet.md)).

## Never do this

- Run a car engine in a garage to recharge, even with the door open: carbon monoxide kills.
- Follow social media nonstop during the crisis.
- Keep the phone as your only light and your only source of information.
- Wait until the battery is empty before looking for a way to recharge.

## Without equipment

- No way to recharge: switch the phone off and turn it on only at set times, for a few minutes, to send and pick up your messages.
- Phone dead for good: use the numbers written on paper and a neighbor's phone.

## Depending on where you are

- **In a car**: plug the phone in while you drive, and keep the power bank for the stops.
- **On a hike**: leave with a full battery and tell someone where you are going. There is not a signal everywhere: a paper map never runs out of power.
- **In a reception center**: charging points are sometimes set up. Ask the local authority.
