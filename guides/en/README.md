# Practical survival guides

[Version française](../fr/README.md)

Short guides to act when things go wrong. Each guide answers one question, starts with the essentials ("In short") and lists its sources.

> **Important**: these guides do not replace instructions from the authorities or first-aid training. In a life-threatening emergency, call your local emergency number (112 in Europe, 911 in North America).

## Read the guides without the app

The guides are plain text files, sorted by theme in this folder. You can:

- read them here, on GitHub, by clicking their title;
- read them in the Survival Shelf app, on the "Guides" page, even offline;
- copy them to your phone, your computer or a USB stick, then open them with any text editor;
- print the ones that matter to you.

To get everything at once, use the "Download all guides" button in the app, or the green "Code" button then "Download ZIP" on the project's GitHub page.

## Water

- [Work out how much water you need](water/work-out-how-much-water-you-need.md): how many liters to plan per person per day.
- [Store water](water/store-water.md): which containers to choose, where to keep them, when to replace the water.
- [Find water at home](water/find-water-at-home.md): water heater, pipes and ice cubes, when the tap runs dry.
- [Find water outdoors](water/find-water-outdoors.md): where to look, which water to choose, what never to drink.
- [Make water safe to drink](water/make-water-safe-to-drink.md): boil it, and what to do when you cannot.
- [Filter water](water/filter-water.md): clear cloudy water, use or build a filter.
- [Disinfect water](water/disinfect-water.md): bleach, tablets or sunlight, with the doses.
- [Collect rainwater](water/collect-rainwater.md): how to catch it, and what it can be used for.
