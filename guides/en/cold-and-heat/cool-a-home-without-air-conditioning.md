# Cool a home without air conditioning

> **In short**: by day, close shutters, blinds and windows before the sun hits them and for as long as it is hotter outside than inside. At night and early in the morning, open wide and create drafts. Turn off everything that gives off heat. A fan does not cool the air: it helps the person it blows on, and no longer helps above 35 °C (95 °F).

## When to use it

- Heatwave, overheating home.
- Air conditioning failure or power outage in summer.

## What you need

- Shutters, blinds or something to shade the windows.
- One thermometer outside and one inside, to know when to open.
- A fan if you have power.
- Cloths and water.

## Steps

1. **Close before the sun arrives.** Shutters, blinds and windows on the sunny side are closed early in the morning, or before you leave. Shading on the outside of the glass (shutters, external blinds) stops heat far better than a curtain inside.
2. **Keep windows closed for as long as it is hotter outside than inside.** Also close the doors between hot rooms and cool rooms.
3. **Open wide as soon as the outside air is cooler**: in the evening, at night, early in the morning.
4. **Create drafts**: open windows on two sides of the home, or one low and one high. Hot air rises and escapes at the top.
5. **Turn off what heats**: lights, computer, television, oven. Cook at cooler times, or eat cold.
6. **Use the fan well.** It only cools the person it blows on: it is useless in an empty room. Above 35 °C (95 °F), it no longer protects against heat illness. Do not point it straight at the body.
7. **Add moisture sparingly.** A damp cloth hung in front of an open window or a wet towel cools the air a little but raises humidity: keep air moving at the same time.
8. **Water in the evening**: terrace, balcony and plants.
9. **Move to the coolest room**, especially to sleep.
10. **Watch the temperature**: WHO advises staying below 32 °C (90 °F) by day and 24 °C (75 °F) at night. If you cannot, spend 2 to 3 hours a day in a cool place.

## Never do this

- Open the windows in the middle of the afternoon "to get some air".
- Rely on a fan alone when it is above 35 °C (95 °F) in the room.
- Leave on appliances you are not using.
- Leave a vulnerable person alone in an overheated home.

## Without equipment

- No shutters: stretch a light-colored sheet on the outside of the window, or failing that close the curtains.
- No draft possible: hang a damp cloth in front of the open window.
- No power: wet your skin and spend the hot hours in a cool place (see [Protect yourself from the heat](protect-yourself-from-the-heat.md)).

## Depending on where you are

- **Windows open at night**: an insect screen or a grille lets you leave them open without worry.
- **House with an upper floor**: sleep downstairs if upstairs stays hot.
