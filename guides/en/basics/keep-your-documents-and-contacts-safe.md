# Keep your documents and contacts safe

> **In short**: copy your important documents and keep the copies in three places: on paper in a waterproof pouch in your go-bag, as files on a USB drive or in online storage, and with someone you trust. Write your contacts on paper: a dead phone gives you no numbers. Add a little cash.

## When to use it

- Now, while things are calm, then once a year to update.
- Before a possible departure: flood warning, fire, evacuation.
- After a disaster, to prove who you are, get care and file insurance claims.

## What you need

- A scanner, or simply the camera of your phone.
- A waterproof pouch, or two zip bags one inside the other.
- A USB drive or an external drive, and online storage if you want.
- A sheet of paper and a pen for the contact list.

## Steps

1. **Gather the documents that identify you**: photo ID, passport, birth and marriage certificates, driver's license, residence permit, pet identification.
2. **Add health**: health insurance card, current prescriptions, list of medications and allergies, immunization records, your doctor's contact details.
3. **Add housing and money**: lease or deed, insurance policies (home, car, health), bank account details, proof of income, latest tax statement.
4. **Photograph or scan every document**, both sides, and check that everything is readable.
5. **Keep the copies in three places**:
   - on paper, in a waterproof pouch, in your go-bag;
   - as files, on a USB drive or an external drive, and in online storage if you wish;
   - with someone you trust or in a safe deposit box, away from your home.
6. **Keep the originals together**, somewhere easy to grab on the way out. If you are ordered to evacuate, take everyone's ID.
7. **Write your contact list on paper**: family, out-of-area contact, doctor, school, employer, insurer, bank, neighbors. Make a card for each member of the household to carry at all times.
8. **Save an "ICE" contact** (In Case of Emergency) in your phone: responders look for it.
9. **Take pictures of your home and belongings**, room by room: they are evidence for your insurer.
10. **Keep a little cash** in small bills: ATMs and cards may stop working.
11. **Add a recent photo of every member of the household** and of every pet: it helps to find each other if you get separated.
12. **Update once a year**, and after every change: new policy, new number, new prescription.

## Never do this

- Keep a single copy, in the same place as the originals.
- Rely on your phone alone for your contacts and documents.
- Store a USB drive with your documents unprotected: encrypt it or protect it with a password, like your online storage.
- Write your card PINs or passwords in clear on the list.

## Without equipment

- No scanner and no printer: photograph the documents with your phone and send them to your own email address and to someone you trust.
- Leaving right now with no copies: take the original IDs, the prescriptions and the medicines. The rest can be replaced.

## Depending on where you are

- **In a flood zone**: keep the originals high up, never in the basement.
- **Abroad**: keep a copy of your passport apart from the original and write down the contact details of your consulate.
- **With children**: slip a card into their bag with their name, the parents' numbers and the out-of-area contact.
