# Pack a go-bag

> **In short**: one backpack per person, packed in advance, with enough for 3 days: water, food that needs no cooking, battery radio and flashlight, first aid kit, medicines, warm clothes, copies of documents, cash, charger. Keep it somewhere easy to reach and check it once a year.

## When to use it

- Now: a bag is packed before you need it.
- When an evacuation is possible: flood warning, wildfire, industrial accident.
- To hold out at home without water or power: the same kit works on the spot.

## What you need

- A sturdy backpack per person, or a wheeled suitcase for those who cannot carry.
- Waterproof bags for documents and matches.
- A label with the date of the last check.

## Steps

1. **Start with water and food.**
   - Water: plan about 4 liters (1 gallon) per person per day, for 3 days, in small bottles (see [Work out how much water you need](../water/work-out-how-much-water-you-need.md)). If the bag gets too heavy, keep at least one day of water in it and the rest next to it.
   - Food that needs neither cooking nor much water: energy bars, dried fruit, canned food, baby food. Add a manual can opener.
2. **Add what keeps you informed and lit**: battery or hand-crank radio, flashlight, spare batteries, phone charger and backup battery.
3. **Add what keeps you healthy**: first aid kit, your usual medicines with the prescriptions (a week's supply if you can), spare glasses, hygiene items.
4. **Add what protects you**: warm clothes, emergency blanket, rain gear, sturdy shoes.
5. **Add the tools**: multi-purpose tool, lighter or matches, whistle, masks, work gloves.
6. **Add documents and money**: copies of essential documents in a waterproof pouch, cash in small bills (ATMs and cards may not work), spare keys for home and car, a contact list on paper (see [Keep your documents and contacts safe](keep-your-documents-and-contacts-safe.md)).
7. **For an evacuation, complete it**: change of clothes, sleeping bag or blanket, paper map of the area.
8. **Adapt it to your household**: diapers and formula for a baby, food and a leash for a pet, hearing aid batteries, games to keep children busy.
9. **Keep the bag near the exit**, somewhere you can find it in the dark, and tell the whole household where it is.
10. **Check it once a year**: expiry dates, batteries, children's clothing sizes. Replace at once whatever you use.

## Never do this

- Pack a bag so heavy that you cannot carry it for long.
- Put the originals of your documents or your only box of medicine in it, then forget it.
- Wait for the alert to pack: on the day, you will only have a few minutes.
- Rely on a bank card alone.

## Without equipment

- No bag ready and you must leave now: take, in this order, ID, medicines and prescriptions, phone and charger, water, a warm layer.
- No budget: start with what you already have (water bottles, canned food, an old flashlight) and add to it little by little.

## Depending on where you are

- **In the car**: in winter, also keep blankets, water, something to eat and a full tank.
- **In a flood zone**: store the bag high up, out of reach of the water.
