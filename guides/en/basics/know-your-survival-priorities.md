# Know your survival priorities

> **In short**: first get out of danger and deal with serious injuries. Then, in this order: shelter from cold, rain or heat, water, a way to be found, and only after that food. A rule of thumb from survival instructors: in freezing weather you last about 3 hours without shelter, 3 days without water, 3 weeks without food.

## When to use it

- Everything stops at once: outage, storm, accident, you are lost or stranded.
- You do not know where to start and you are afraid to forget something vital.
- To build your stock and your go-bag in the right order.

## Steps

1. **Stop.** Sit down, breathe, look around. Panic is your greatest enemy: a few minutes of thinking prevent serious mistakes (see [Stay calm and decide under stress](stay-calm-and-decide-under-stress.md)).
2. **Move away from immediate danger**: fire, smoke, rising water, a damaged building, traffic.
3. **Deal with serious injuries** and call for help if you can (see [Call the emergency services](call-the-emergency-services.md)).
4. **Protect your body from cold, wind, rain or sun.** This is the first emergency as soon as the weather is bad: in freezing temperatures, count about 3 hours without shelter. Put on dry clothes, get out of the wind, insulate yourself from the ground (see [Protect yourself from the cold](../cold-and-heat/protect-yourself-from-the-cold.md) and [Build an emergency shelter](../cold-and-heat/build-an-emergency-shelter.md)).
5. **Find water**: about 3 days without drinking, much less in hot weather or when you work hard. Start with your reserve, then see [Find water at home](../water/find-water-at-home.md) and [Make water safe to drink](../water/make-water-safe-to-drink.md).
6. **Make yourself findable and stay informed**: tell someone where you are, listen to the radio, make yourself visible if people are looking for you.
7. **Light a fire only if it helps**: to warm up, boil water or signal your position (see [Light a fire](../fire-and-power/light-a-fire.md)).
8. **Think about food last**: you last about 3 weeks without eating. Ration what you have, but take no risk to find food.
9. **Review the situation regularly.** The order depends on what is happening: in a heatwave, water and shade come before everything; with a casualty, calling for help comes before shelter.

These durations are rules of thumb, not guarantees: they change with temperature, effort, age and health.

## Never do this

- Go looking for food when you have neither shelter nor water.
- Walk at night, exhausted or injured, without knowing where you are going: stay put.
- Drink alcohol to warm up: it makes you lose heat faster.
- Eat snow to quench your thirst: it cools the body. Melt it first.

## Depending on where you are

- **At home**: you already have shelter. The priorities become water, warmth, information and medicines. Official emergency kits aim for at least 3 days of self-sufficiency.
- **In the car, in winter**: stay in the car: it is your shelter (see [Protect yourself from the cold](../cold-and-heat/protect-yourself-from-the-cold.md)).
- **Outdoors, lost**: stay where you are as soon as night falls, or if you are injured or exhausted. Drink, rest before you are worn out, and fix small problems right away.
- **In hot weather**: shade and water come first (see [Protect yourself from the heat](../cold-and-heat/protect-yourself-from-the-heat.md)).
