# Stay calm and decide under stress

> **In short**: stop before you act. Breathe slowly for a few minutes, press your feet into the ground, look at what is around you, then decide one thing at a time. Panic does more damage than most dangers: remember STOP, for Stop, Think, Observe, Plan.

## When to use it

- Your heart is racing, your thoughts go round in circles, you no longer know what to do.
- You must decide fast: go or stay, call or wait.
- Someone near you is panicking.

## Steps

1. **Stop.** Unless the danger is immediate, do not move right away. Sit down if you can.
2. **Breathe slowly.** Breathe in gently through your nose, counting to 5, letting the air flow down toward your belly, then breathe out through your mouth, counting to 5. Keep going for a few minutes. If you feel dizzy, breathe less deeply.
3. **Come back into your body.** Push your feet into the floor, stretch your arms or press your hands together.
4. **Come back to the present.** Name 5 things you can see, 3 or 4 things you can hear, what you can smell. Touch your knees or an object. This does not make the storm go away, but it stops you from being swept off.
5. **Think.** Where are you? What really threatens you, right now? What do you have: people, equipment, water, shelter, ways to call for help?
6. **Observe.** The weather, the time of day, how everyone is doing, the landmarks around you.
7. **Plan.** List your options and choose the safest. If you are not confident, if night is falling, if you are injured or exhausted: stay where you are.
8. **Act on one task only**, with your full attention. Then move to the next.
9. **Look after the machine.** Drink, eat a little, rest before you are exhausted, fix small problems (a blister, wet clothing) before they grow.
10. **Review** as soon as the situation changes.

## Never do this

- Run or set off straight ahead without a specific reason.
- Make a big decision at the peak of panic, if it can wait two minutes.
- Blame yourself for being afraid: fear is normal, panic is what you are trying to avoid.

## Without equipment

Everything above needs nothing at all. Practice for one or two minutes now and then, when all is well: on the day you need it, it will come by itself.

## Depending on where you are

- **Lost outdoors**: do not move until you have a specific reason to. Try to remember the way you came and look for landmarks.
- **In a group**: watch for signs of distress in others: indecision, withdrawal, forgetfulness, carelessness.
