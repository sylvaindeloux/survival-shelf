# Shelf lives of the built-in catalog

When you add a lot of a catalog product without typing its date, the app estimates the expiry from the
typical shelf life below: today plus that many months, at month precision (`YYYY-MM`). The date printed on
the packaging is always more reliable, and you can change the estimate at any time.

Values are for the unopened product, sold in France, stored cool, dry and dark. Most French figures come
from manufacturers' technical sheets (the "total shelf life" published by the food distributor Pomona),
checked against ANSES, DGCCRF, EU law, USDA and US university extensions. Research done in October 2026.

## What the date means

- Most foods carry a **best-before date** (DDM, "à consommer de préférence avant"). It is about quality, not
  safety: past it, food is still safe to eat if the packaging is intact and it looks and smells normal
  ([ANSES](https://www.anses.fr/fr/content/date-limite-de-consommation-dlc-et-date-de-durabilite-minimale-ddm),
  [DGCCRF](https://www.economie.gouv.fr/particuliers/mes-droits-conso/alimentation/date-limite-de-consommation-dlc-date-de-durabilite)).
- **Medicines and purification tablets** have a real expiry date: do not rely on them past it.
- Throw away bulging, leaking, rusted or deeply dented cans, whatever the date.
- Among foods, only **solid sugar and cooking salt** are exempt from any date
  ([EU Regulation 1169/2011, Annex X](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/HTML/?uri=CELEX:32011R1169)).
  Honey is not: its best-before date is mandatory
  ([DGCCRF](https://www.economie.gouv.fr/dgccrf/les-fiches-pratiques/etiquetage-du-miel)).

## Things that do not expire but need a check

| Product | What to do | Sources |
|---|---|---|
| Water filter | Check it after 5 years. Never let it freeze once used. | [Sawyer](https://www.sawyer.com/faqs), [LifeStraw](https://help.lifestraw.com/en-US/do-lifestraw-filters-expire-4012928) |
| First aid kit | Check its contents every year. | [St John Ambulance](https://shop.sja.org.uk/pages/do-first-aid-kits-expire) |
| Gas cartridge | The gas keeps but the seals age: replace after about 7 years. Only source found: a Japanese industry guideline. | [Japanese guideline](https://tetsudo-ch.com/12913823.html) |
| Power bank, radio battery | Recharge every 3 months, store about half charged. | [Anker](https://service.anker.com/eu/article-description/How-do-I-store-my-Anker-power-bank-when-it-will-not-be-used-for-an-extended-period-of-time), [Battery University](https://batteryuniversity.com/article/bu-702-how-to-store-batteries) |
| Headlamp, radio | Store without batteries. | [Duracell](https://www.duracell-me.com/help/storage) |
| Camping stove | Check the seals before use. | [Japanese guideline](https://tetsudo-ch.com/12913823.html) |
| Jerrycan | Water you fill yourself is renewed every 6 months. | [CDC](https://cdc.gov/water-emergency/about/how-to-create-and-store-an-emergency-water-supply.html) |

Toilet paper, candles, matches (kept dry), bin bags, survival blanket, can opener and multi-tool do not
expire. No source was found for most of them: this is common sense for things stored dry.
